Partner restaurants

Two kinds of places. Spots for captains: restaurants the table’s captain books under their own name, through OpenTable, Resy, Tock, the restaurant’s site or a call, then tells Charlie the time and the name. Hash Table never contacts them, so add a booking link or a phone number and one line about what it’s like. Partners: restaurants that agreed to hold tables for Hash Table, so nothing needs booking. Nothing is booked in a restaurant’s own system by the app itself: OpenTable, Resy and the rest have no open booking API. Each partner agrees to hold a few tables on certain evenings. A standing hold is set unless the restaurant says it can’t; a partner that confirms each evening has to say yes first. Restaurants get the week’s plan on Sunday at 10 PM (with what to release), the final count at noon the day before (the same moment members stop changing plans for free), and any change after that. They see sizes, times, dietary notes and the captain’s first name, never who’s coming. Backup partners are used last.

Real restaurants with their OpenTable, Resy or Tock links, food type, whether they take six online, and how easy it is to talk there. Already added ones are brought up to date, with the spot checks we did.

Needs a person

Is there a table for six?

OpenTable, Resy and Tock don’t share availability, and reading their pages by machine is against their terms, so people look, and it costs nothing. Captains tell the app what a booking page showed when they come back from it, and every captain that night sees it right away. This tab fills the gaps: on Sunday night, once the week is out, open the places at the top of each night (the ones nobody has looked at since yesterday, quietest first), look at the times for six around 7, and tap what you saw. About ten seconds a place, and ten places a night is plenty. If a night is still bare at 8:30 PM on Sunday, whoever is on duty gets a note.

Add a spot check (what a booking page showed for six on one evening)

Open each place’s OpenTable or Resy page for six at 7:00 on the evening and note the times it offers near 7. Paste them as {"date": "2026-10-30", "size": 6, "seen": {"The Maharaja": ["18:45", "19:00", "19:15"], "Harvest": [], "La Fábrica Central": "call"}}. An empty list means nothing for six from 6 to 8. Captains see when each place was looked at, and places found full drop to the bottom of their list.
