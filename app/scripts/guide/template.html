Where to book your table of six

Restaurants between HBS, Harvard and MIT, picked for six people who want to talk. Set your night and where everyone’s coming from, and the list sorts itself, quiet rooms and fair trips first. Every booking button opens with your night, time and party filled in

This is research, not live availability. It tells you which rooms take a table of six and how an evening usually goes. The booking page has the final word on what’s free

  1. Pick from the top of the list

    It’s sorted for your table: a room where six can talk, tables that seat six, an evening that’s usually easy to book, and a trip that’s fair to everyone. The person coming from farthest counts most

  2. Open its booking page

    The button opens OpenTable, Resy or Tock with your night, time and party size already in. What you see there is what’s really free

  3. No 7:00? Anything from 6:00 to 8:00 works

    Take the time closest to your table’s. If nothing shows for six, try the next place on the list instead of a smaller table, since the booking won’t stretch later

  4. Six won’t go online? Call

    Some places only take five or fewer online, or book groups by phone. The script on this page has your night filled in

  5. Leave a note for the table you want

    Paste the booking note into the special requests box. Each place card says which table to ask for, like a booth away from the bar or the room by the fireplace

  6. Check the fee before you confirm

    For six, many places hold a card and charge per person if you cancel late, often inside 24 to 48 hours. A few add a tip for groups. Book only one place

  7. Tell Charlie it’s booked

    In the app, tap I booked it and add the time and the name it’s under. If a place was full, say so there too, and the other captains that night skip it

When it’s full everywhere

  • Weeknights are much easier than Friday and Saturday, so try another night with your table if you can
  • Places with a private room or lots of tables for six free up more often
  • Book a few days ahead. Some places only open bookings three days out
